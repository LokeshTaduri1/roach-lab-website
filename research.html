      Our science

      Research

      We study the full experience of sickle cell disease pain: biological, psychological, social and systemic.

      Overview

      Why pain in sickle cell disease?

      Pain is the hallmark of sickle cell disease and the leading reason people seek emergency care. Yet it is still under-studied and often under-treated. Our research looks for practical ways to measure, understand and relieve that pain.

      01
      Area 01

      Pain Mechanisms & Phenotyping

      People with SCD experience pain very differently. We describe patterns of acute vaso-occlusive pain and chronic pain to understand why some people go on to develop persistent, widespread pain.

      • Acute crisis pain and the transition to chronic pain
      • Pain sensitivity and central sensitization
      • Pain trajectories across adolescence and adulthood
      02
      Area 02

      Biobehavioral Factors

      Pain does not happen on its own. We look at how sleep, stress, mood, fatigue and coping interact with SCD pain, and how these factors could be targets for intervention.

      • Sleep quality and next-day pain
      • Stress, depression, anxiety and resilience
      • Coping strategies and pain-related functioning
      03
      Area 03

      Patient-Reported Outcomes & Digital Health

      Patients know their pain best. We build and test patient-centered measures and mobile tools that capture symptoms in daily life and support self-management.

      • Daily pain diaries and ecological momentary assessment
      • Mobile health tools for symptom tracking
      • Community-engaged design with patients and families
      04
      Area 04

      Health Equity & Access to Care

      People with SCD, most of whom are Black, often face stigma and long waits for pain treatment. We study these barriers and test ways to make care more timely, respectful and fair.

      • Stigma and bias in emergency and inpatient pain care
      • Transition from pediatric to adult care
      • Community partnerships and patient advocacy
      Approach

      How we work

      We combine several methods so we can see the full picture of pain.

      Quantitative studies

      Longitudinal cohorts, validated questionnaires and statistical modeling of pain outcomes.

      Qualitative research

      Interviews and focus groups that capture what living with SCD pain is really like.

      Community engagement

      Partnerships with patients, families, clinicians and advocacy organizations at every stage.

      Interested in collaborating?

      We welcome partnerships with clinicians, researchers and community organizations working in sickle cell disease.
