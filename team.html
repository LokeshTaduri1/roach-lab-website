      Our Team

      Principal Investigator

      Keesha Powell-Roach, PhD, RN, FAAN

      Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor (Tenure-Track), Division of Nursing Science, Rutgers School of Nursing

      Dr. Powell-Roach studies pain in people living with sickle cell disease (SCD). Her work combines genomic, proteomic, psychological and environmental data to understand what drives acute and chronic pain, and she is committed to reducing health disparities through personalized, culturally sensitive care.

      Research interests: acute and chronic pain, sickle cell disease, biobehavioral and omics integration, health disparities.

      Education: PhD, University of Illinois Chicago · Postdoctoral training, University of Florida
