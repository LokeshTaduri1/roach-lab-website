      Principal Investigator

      Dr. Keesha Roach

      Rutgers University

      Dr. Keesha Roach leads the Powell-Roach Lab at Rutgers University. Her research focuses on pain in sickle cell disease: how it is experienced, measured and treated. She aims to improve quality of life and health equity for people living with SCD.

      Her work draws on clinical, behavioral and community-engaged methods to create patient-centered strategies for assessing and managing acute and chronic pain.
