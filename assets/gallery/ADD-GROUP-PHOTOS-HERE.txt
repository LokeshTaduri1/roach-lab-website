Group photos in this folder:
  group-1.jpg, group-2.jpg, group-3.jpg

- All three appear in the full-width slider on the HOME page (above 'About the lab').
- group-1.jpg is also the big banner photo at the top of the JOIN THE LAB page.

On big screens each photo sits in the centre (up to 1200px wide) with a blurred copy filling both sides.
Keep the file name in the strip-bg style the same as the img src. To change which part stays in view, edit the
object-position % on its img in index.html (lower % = show more of the top).
Resize large phone photos to about 1600-1920 px on the long side before adding them.
Edit captions in index.html (search for 'strip-slide').
To add another photo, copy one <figure class="strip-slide"> block in index.html.
