      Join our team or plan your next research collaboration with us:

      Please note: We welcome inquiries from prospective students, trainees, collaborators and research volunteers throughout the year.

      The Powell-Roach Lab is a collaborative research group at Rutgers University. We study pain in sickle cell disease and work to improve care, quality of life and health equity. We are always looking to work alongside motivated people, subject experts and diverse thinkers.

      For undergraduate students

      Gain hands-on research experience in data collection, literature reviews, study coordination and community outreach. Positions may be available for course credit or as volunteer roles. No prior research experience is needed; we provide training.

      For graduate students

      Prospective master's and doctoral students interested in pain science, sickle cell disease, patient-reported outcomes or health equity are encouraged to reach out before applying to their program.

      For postdoctoral fellows

      We support postdocs in building independent research programs, publishing, and applying for fellowship and career-development funding.

      For clinicians, researchers and community partners

      Collaborate with us on sickle cell disease pain, biobehavioral factors such as sleep and stress, digital symptom tracking, and equitable access to pain care. We also welcome partnerships with community and advocacy organizations.

      Volunteer to participate in our research

      If you or a family member lives with sickle cell disease and you'd like to learn about our current studies, please contact us. A team member will explain eligibility and what taking part involves. Participation is always voluntary.

      How to get in touch

      Send us a short note that includes:

      • Your background and research interests
      • Why you'd like to join or collaborate with the lab
      • Your availability, plus a current CV or résumé
